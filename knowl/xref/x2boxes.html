Example 7.106 boxes.


A shaded square tilted \(45\) degrees, with bottom corner \((1,0)\) and top corner \((1,2)\text{.}\) Its edges from the bottom corner are the arrows \(\sqrt{2} e_1\) and \(\sqrt{2} e_2\text{.}\)
🔗
The box \((1,0) + P( \sqrt{2} e_1,\sqrt{2} e_2 )\text{,}\) where \(e_1 = ( \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} )\) and \(e_2 = ( -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} )\text{.}\)
🔗

A rectangular box in three dimensions, whose edges from one corner are the arrows \(e_1\text{,}\) \(2e_2\) and \(e_3\text{,}\) so it is twice as long in the direction of \(e_2\text{.}\)
🔗
The box \(P(e_1,2e_2,e_3)\text{,}\) where \(e_1, e_2,e_3\) is the standard basis of \(\R3\text{.}\)
